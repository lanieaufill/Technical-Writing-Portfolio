# Laptop Update Guide for Public Access Laptops

### Who is this for:

This is for administrators and employees that are responsible for maintaining laptop usability on public access hardware. 

### When to use this document: 

This is to be used during monthly laptop updates. Managers will be notified of the timeframe in which the updates will take place monthly, as well as a time frame to have the set-up and take-down complete within. IT will inform you if a specific laptop was unable to access the network, and remind you to include it in the next month's update.
